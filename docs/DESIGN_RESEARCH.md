# TargetJEE Engine — design research and decisions

Research synthesis completed 16 November 2026, before frontend components or styles were written. This is a design rationale, **not evidence that this particular app improves examination scores or retention**. Product hypotheses require learner studies. No universal hue has been proven to increase retention; contrast, task demands, ambient illumination and personal preference matter more than marketing claims about colour psychology.

## Palette tokens

The complete semantic palette deliberately excludes blue-green hues. Alerts use clay; rewards use leaf/forest; insight uses plum. Graphs always pair colour with labels/shapes. Tokens are shared by CSS and audited tests. All ordinary copy in high contrast uses ≥7:1 pairs; disabled controls retain readable text.

Light-mode hero surfaces use moss `#e6ecd9`, apricot `#f4e6d9` and lilac `#ede7f2`; their text stays dark, never low-contrast white. Dark and high-contrast modes use their corresponding semantic surfaces. The colour family is an aesthetic choice; it is not assigned medical or cognitive efficacy. Hover does not introduce a new hue. There are no forbidden utility colour families.

## Four unresolved template fields: explicit defaults, not invented user inputs

The request literally contains `[FONT_STYLE]`, `[SWITCHDESIGNPROMPT]`, `[LANDINGPAGEPROMPT]`, and `[THINGSTOSHOWINDASHBOARD]` without supplied values. Exact compliance with absent specifications cannot be verified. The zero-intervention instruction means the following defaults are implemented and replace, rather than pretend to satisfy, those placeholders:

- **Font:** self-hosted variable **Manrope** for interface/body; **Newsreader** for large editorial accents. Global CSS variables `--font-ui` and `--font-editorial`. No external font requests or prohibited font families.
- **Switch:** a tactile three-position, icon-labelled segmented capsule for Light / Dark / High contrast, persisted under `targetjee.theme.v1`; icon-only controls have full accessible labels.
- **Landing:** a warm, editorial study workspace with a left product rail, exam selector, a large split hero, five-second trap card, mastery pathway, three feature modules and clear drill/forge entry points. Bounded procedural orbital visual references the PDF without making reading depend on WebGL.
- **Dashboard:** score and recoverable marks, explicitly provisional estimate stability, pacing stage, mastery path, weekly activity, subject breakdown, recent drills, review-depth switch, replay, answer-change evidence, flashcards, diagnostic model details, AI coach, import/export/reset and privacy controls.

## Interaction contract and evaluation

- Native smooth scrolling; all animation/scrolling respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Explicit 480/768/1024/1440px media queries. Wrapping options and scroll-contained KaTeX prevent document overflow.
- Keyboard-accessible modal with focus trap, escape/close and focus restoration; logical headings, skip link and form errors.
- No inference of student intent from clicks. Timing categories are observable patterns with learner confirmation.
- Confidence calibration uses **only explicit diagnostic tags**; the PDF's proposed behavioral confidence inference is intentionally not used because it would falsely attribute certainty.
- Seed item IRT/NRM parameters are illustrative, not population calibrated. Short or low-information drills always show a provisional badge. No AIR, percentile, causal learning-gain or “true ability” claims.
- Proposed research: measure task completion, delayed transfer to novel problems, reported workload, visual comfort and voluntary return. Compare bounded repair flow with an answer-key baseline, obtain consent and avoid collecting keys or private textbook scans.
